01 Membership

Any Chartered Accountant of ICAN Nigeria living, working or studying in the UK. We welcome newly qualified ACAs to Fellows (FCA), plus student members on the ICAN pathway. You must be in good standing with ICAN Nigeria.

Apply via Membership Portal — verification via FCA check and SSO.

Annual subscription covers access to 12+ CPD programmes, monthly meetings, mentorship matching, benevolence fund eligibility, Member Hub (certificates, directory, magazine archive), and discounted Annual Conference & Gala Dinner tickets.

Fees are reviewed annually by the Executive Committee. Contact Financial Secretary for current rates.

Our Welfare Officer (Mrs Ngozi M. — Compassionate member support) runs integration clinics: career guidance, accommodation pointers, and a buddy system linking new arrivals with established members in London, Birmingham, Manchester & Scotland.

Visit Member Hub → SSO login with FCA-verified email. You get CPD tracking, event registration, benevolence forms, directory and magazine archive. Encrypted and FCA Verified.

Yes. We run mentorship from student to Fellowship, CPD for exam preparation, and welfare support. Contact Membership Secretary: Ifeoma Okafor, FCA.

02 CPD & Professional Development

12+ structured programmes annually — technical masterclasses, leadership pipelines, HMRC policy updates, Big Four insights. Most are verifiable CPD and certificates are auto-issued in Member Hub.

Monthly meetings are hybrid (London + Teams). Annual Conference is in-person flagship in London (24 Oct). CPD portal has on-demand archive.

Technical Secretary: Samuel Olayioye, FCA — rigour in technical delivery. Plus guest speakers from Big Four, HMRC, ICAN Nigeria leadership and sponsors like KPMG, Deloitte, Sage.

Yes. Our certificates map to ICAN Nigeria CPD framework and are recognised for UK professional development records. Contact General Secretary: Olajumoke Sangojinmi, FCA.
